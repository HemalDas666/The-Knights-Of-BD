Server rules

Short version: don't ruin someone else's game. The long version is below — by joining the server you agree to all of it.

1. Respect every player

Trash talk in PvP is fine. Hate is not.

  • No racism, sexism, homophobia or slurs of any kind.
  • No targeted harassment, doxxing or threats.
  • No spamming chat, caps flooding or microphone abuse.

2. No cheating or unfair advantage

Anything that plays the game for you is a ban.

  • No X-ray, fly, reach, kill aura, auto-clickers or macros.
  • No exploiting bugs, duplication glitches or chunk resets.
  • Found a bug? Report it in Discord — reporting is rewarded, abusing is punished.

3. Griefing and stealing

Survival is dangerous — but some places are off limits.

  • Spawn, hubs, event arenas and claimed land are fully protected.
  • Destroying builds outside PvP areas is not allowed.
  • Stealing from unclaimed land is part of survival — claim your base.

4. One account, one player

  • No alt accounts to bypass a ban, mute or cooldown.
  • No ban evasion through friends' accounts.
  • Sharing an account is your own risk — staff will not restore lost items.

5. No advertising

  • No advertising other servers, Discords or services in chat.
  • No links to scams, phishing sites or key generators.

6. Don't lag the server

  • No lag machines, redstone clocks, mob cramming or chunk loaders.
  • Keep entity counts reasonable — staff may remove builds that hurt performance.

7. Staff have the final say

If a staff member asks you to stop, stop. Argue in a Discord ticket, not in chat.

  • Impersonating staff is an instant ban.
  • False reports waste everyone's time and can be punished.

Store & purchase policy

  • All prices are charged in BDT (৳) only.
  • Purchases are delivered in-game to the Minecraft IGN saved on your account. Double-check it before buying.
  • Ranks sent to the wrong IGN cannot be moved or refunded.
  • Delivery is manual — usually within minutes, but allow up to 24 hours during busy periods.
  • Chargebacks and payment fraud result in a permanent ban and rank removal.
  • Bought the wrong item? Open a Discord ticket within 24 hours and staff will help if it has not been delivered.
Questions about a rule? Open a ticket in the Discord server before you act, not after.
